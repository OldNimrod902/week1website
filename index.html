About Fortnite Islands

Islands are the primary battlegrounds of Fortnite.

In Battle Royale there is a single island availble at a time, that gets updated every season (sometimes mid-season for events) and gets entirely replaced at the end of the every chapter.

In Reload and Blitz modes have a map pool with multiple maps being available at the same time.

Various maps have different art styles; in chapters 1-3 the maps had more cartoonish artstyle, while since chapter 4 the maps aim for realism.

Fun fact!

Up until chapter 6, all battle royale maps were named after greek mythology characters.
